# 📊 Streaming Platform Churn Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Overview

This project analyzes customer churn behavior for a subscription-based streaming platform.
The goal is to identify key factors that influence customer retention and provide actionable insights to reduce churn.

The analysis focuses on customer demographics, subscription plans, pricing, and service usage patterns.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Identify which customers are most likely to churn
* Understand the impact of contract type and pricing on churn
* Analyze customer behavior based on tenure and service usage
* Provide insights to improve customer retention

---

## 🧾 Dataset

The dataset contains customer-level subscription data, including:

* Customer ID
* Demographics (Gender, Senior Citizen, Dependents)
* Subscription details (Contract type, Payment method)
* Usage behavior (Streaming services, Tech support)
* Financials (Monthly Charges, Total Charges)
* Tenure (duration of subscription)
* Churn (0 = Stayed, 1 = Left)

## 🔍 Key Insights

* Customers on **month-to-month contracts** show significantly higher churn
* **Higher monthly charges** are associated with increased churn
* Customers with **short tenure** are more likely to leave
* Certain **payment methods** show higher churn tendencies
* Long-term customers contribute more to overall revenue

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* Encourage users to switch to **long-term contracts**
* Improve **early-stage customer engagement**
* Optimize pricing strategies for high-risk segments
* Provide better support and incentives for new users
